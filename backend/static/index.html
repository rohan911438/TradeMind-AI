The three-engine architecture

Observe. Understand. Protect.

Each engine has a single, clear responsibility. Together they turn a raw tick stream into a risk-checked decision.

01 · Data engine

StreamX

Captures every tick and order-book update with nanosecond timestamps, keeps real-time market state and records history to Parquet.

  • →Event bus (Kafka-compatible)
  • →Order-book imbalance & volatility
  • →P50 / P95 / P99 latency monitor
02 · AI engine

PredictX

An XGBoost model trained on real NSE price history produces BUY / SELL / HOLD / NO TRADE signals and explains why.

  • →10 technical features
  • →Per-prediction factor contributions
  • →Book pressure + news sentiment blend
03 · Risk engine

RiskGuard

A promising prediction never becomes a risky trade automatically. Every order is approved, reduced or rejected, with reasons.

  • →ATR stop-loss & 1% risk sizing
  • →Risk/reward gate
  • →Exposure caps & daily loss limit
How it works

From tick to trade in five steps

  1. 01

    Stream

    StreamX ingests price, quantity, bid and ask updates.

  2. 02

    Process

    Market state, candles, imbalance and volatility update in microseconds.

  3. 03

    Predict

    PredictX scores the setup and explains the top drivers.

  4. 04

    Guard

    RiskGuard sizes the position and checks every limit.

  5. 05

    Paper trade

    Execute with virtual money and track live P&L.
